Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

Rekalp ("the App") is operated by BRT Digital Media ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and where it's stored. Your account data syncs securely to our servers so it's available across your devices.

1. Information we collect

Account information: your email address, used for login, plus the profile details you provide (name, date of birth, gender, preferred unit system, language, and start-of-week preference).

Fitness data: the workouts, exercises (including any custom exercises you create), training notes, personal records, weekly goals, and body measurements you choose to log.

Subscription data: purchase receipts and verification data processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play to confirm your Rekalp Pro status. We never see or store your payment card details. Apple and Google handle payment directly.

Usage data: anonymized analytics about how the app is used, collected via Amplitude. This does not include your name or personal data.

Advertising data: on the free tier, ads are served via Google Mobile Ads, which may use device identifiers to deliver and measure ads.

Profile picture: currently stored only on your device and never uploaded to our servers. This may change in a future update, in which case we will update this policy.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's core features and keep your training history, exercises, personal records, and measurements in sync across your devices
  • To verify and manage your subscription
  • To improve the app using aggregated, anonymized analytics
  • To contact you about your account (e.g. login codes, important service updates)

3. Where your data is stored

Your account and fitness data are stored on secure cloud servers and also kept on your device for offline access, synced between the two. Your profile picture currently stays device-only and is not sent to our servers; this may change in a future update.

4. Third parties we work with

  • Apple App Store / Google Play: subscription purchase verification
  • Amplitude: anonymized product analytics
  • Google Mobile Ads: ad delivery (free tier only)

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

5. Data retention & account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app (Profile → Danger Zone). Deleting your account removes your personal information from our systems, but it does not cancel an active subscription. To stop future billing, cancel your subscription through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time through the app, or by emailing us at the address below.

7. Children's privacy

Rekalp is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of storage or transmission is ever 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above and, where appropriate, notified through the app or its store listing.

9. Contact
