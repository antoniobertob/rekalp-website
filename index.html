What's inside

Everything you need to train with intent

No spreadsheets, no guessing what you lifted last time.

Workout planner & calendar

Plan a session ahead of time, or start logging on the spot. Resume exactly where you left off if you get interrupted mid-workout.

Full exercise catalog

Hundreds of exercises tagged by muscle group, equipment, and category — plus your own custom moves when the catalog doesn't cover it.

Personal records

Rekalp notices when you beat a lift automatically while you're training, or you can log a record yourself whenever you want.

Stats & measurements

Weekly and monthly training stats, a weekly goal you set for yourself, and body measurements including BMI tracked over time.

Built-in timers

Chrono, AMRAP, Interval, and EMOM timers, right alongside your workout — no separate stopwatch app needed.

Weight calculator

Work out plate loading and percentages straight from your own logged numbers, not a generic chart.
